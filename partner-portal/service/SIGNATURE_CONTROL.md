# SHIFT partner signature control v1

Control reference: `SHIFT-PARTNER-ES256-CONTROL-V1`.

This identifies the software verification controls. It does not certify a provider, approve an assessment or authorise SHIFT staff to prescribe. The treating registered service owns the clinical decision, protocol, prescribing entitlement and lawful use of its signature.

The service requires current professional verification and prescribing scope, approved provider and pharmacy organisations, an assigned current assessment, independent identity and measurement checks, the recorded clinical handover, settled payment and the member's explicit agreement to the current treatment and price. The adult-service and insulin exclusions remain enforced on the server.

A signing key requires verified user presence and verification on the partner origin, with a separate qualified governor's recorded approval and review expiry. A prescriber cannot approve their own key. Challenges bind the session, prescriber, exact document digest and request version; they expire and are single-use. Counters, organisation/profile versions and access are rechecked before the signing transaction commits.

The signed document, proof, key identity, policy reference and audit are retained privately. Registering a pharmacy task, its receipt, preparation, dispatch and delivery are separate confirmations. No message, refund, additional charge or external provider request is automatically executed by these controls.

Historical HQ signatures remain verifiable only as historical evidence against their original allowed origin. New signing challenges use the partner origin. Named qualified partner approval remains mandatory before an individual can use any clinical capability.
